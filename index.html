Frequently Asked Questions

I'm locked out of my car, can you help me?
Yes. We carry specialist kit to get you safely back into your car, even if your keys are locked in the ignition or in the boot. Give us a call and tell us where your keys are so we know exactly what we're dealing with, and we'll be on our way.
How quickly can you reach me?
In most cases we're with you within 15-30 minutes across Belfast and the surrounding areas. For 24/7 emergencies, call us and we'll give you an honest arrival time on the spot.
Can you help if I've lost all my keys?
Yes. Even with no working key, our mobile locksmith can gain entry, cut a new key, and program it to your car on-site, usually in under an hour.
Do you work on my vehicle make and model?
We cover virtually all car makes and models, including BMW, Audi, Mercedes, Volkswagen, Ford, and many more. Tell us your registration and we'll confirm straight away.
Do you use genuine keys or cheap copies?
We only fit high-quality keys and fobs, either genuine or premium OEM-equivalent. Each one is cut and programmed to your exact vehicle, so it looks, feels, and works just like your original. No flimsy copies, and every key we supply is backed by our 12-month guarantee.
How much does a replacement car key cost?
Prices depend on your vehicle make, model, and key type. We always confirm a clear, fixed price upfront before any work begins, with no call-out fee and no hidden extras.
Will you damage my car getting in?
No. We use professional, non-destructive methods to open your car and to cut and program keys, so there's no damage to your locks, doors, or ignition. It's worth asking anyone cheaper exactly how they get in, because forcing or drilling a lock can leave you with a repair bill far bigger than any saving. And we're fully insured, just in case.

Because not every locksmith is the same, and you're trusting someone with your car. Some turn up uninsured, force or damage the lock, fit an unproven key, or disappear if it goes wrong. With KeyFixer you get a fully qualified, fully insured local Belfast locksmith, non-destructive entry, a 12-month guarantee, and 5.0 stars from real local drivers.
